THE MATHPILLAR ADVANTAGE

Why learn with us?

We don’t teach math as a checklist of procedures. We cultivate mathematicians who think critically, enjoy challenge, and know how to approach unfamiliar problems.

⌘

Understanding Over Memorization

Students learn where formulas originate, dissect geometric proofs, and identify invariants instead of relying on blind rote drilling.

◎

Contest & Olympiad Pedagogy

Preparation tailored for AMC 8/10/12 and advanced problem solving, with structured heuristic approaches to non-routine problems.

◉

Small classes create room for face-to-face discussion, individual attention, questions, and mathematical conversation.

✦

Sefa Ozkan is an experienced mathematics educator with a strong background in teaching Algebra, middle school mathematics, and advanced math courses. He also has experience teaching Algebra and advanced mathematics at Alpha Public Schools and creating engaging, critical-thinking-focused lessons at Bay Area Technology School. In addition to his classroom experience, Sefa is the Founder and Coordinator of First Bricks Online Academy, an international online education platform serving students from diverse backgrounds.
